_Content note: Violent death_

---
I often spend time thinking on just how I'll finally go

Whether it's done quick or is, instead, painful and slow
<!--more-->

Whether burning fire, drowned in floods, or freezing snow

Or shot point blank, or lynched, alone in jail, or on death row

<br>

There's one thing that I never think will be my fated end

It seems a foolish dream, given what's coming round the bend

And yet I still will dream, will hope, will wish, and yes, pretend

I'll go when old, in peaceful sleep, 'round family and friends

---
Irshaad Poetry prompt: "How I'd like to die".
