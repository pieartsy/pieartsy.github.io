title: The old book smell

Pluck out your favorite book from the cabinet

with thick rubber gloves.

Ignore the smell.
<!--more-->

Zip it in a sandwich bag to ensure the rot

doesn't reach you.

Ignore the smell.

Adjust the straps on your N95 so you don't get sick.

Don't adjust your hazmat suit, even as the tropical heat

crawls up your neck and flares up excema you thought

you'd grown out of.

Be thankful the cabinet had its glass doors shut,

hopefully minimizing the contamination risk,

ignore the gaping hole cut out of the flooring,

ignore the missing couch covered in fluids,

ignore the infestation of flies,

and for the love of god,

ignore the smell.

Drive the book hundreds of miles in a garbage bag,

first double-checking that it's not the one you threw out

with your teddy bear in it, cherished and

decaying in an anonymous landfill,

and place it in your freezer to eradicate the bacteria

that cause the smell.

Nestle it on your bookshelf in a sacred spot, right in the middle.

Ignore the book.

It smells.

Irshaad Poetry prompt: "Saying goodbye to your childhood home".
