I tell you, [hope has feathers](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/emily-dickinson/hope-is-the-thing-with-feathers/),

[each day blinks a golden eye](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/francis-william-bourdillon/the-night-has-a-thousand-eyes/),

[men are just like continents](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/john-donne/no-man-is-an-island/)--

no need to ask me why.

<br>

I promise: [love's a rooted plant](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/john-boyle-oreilly/a-kiss/),

[a symphony](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/elizabeth-ann-armstrong/love/), [a briar](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/emily-bronte/love-and-friendship/),

[an angel](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/henry-van-dyke/the-prison-and-the-angel/)--no--[a broken gate](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/rupert-brooke/love/)!--

Don't you call me a liar!

<br>

I swear! [Fog does has cat feet](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/45032/fog-56d2245d7b36c),

[bird wings do beat back the night](https://allpoetry.com/poem/8501131-Dawn-by-William-Carlos-Williams),

[Death does ride in a carriage](https://discoverpoetry.com/poems/emily-dickinson/because-i-could-not-stop-for-death/)--

What? Don't you think I'm right?
