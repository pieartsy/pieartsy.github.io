"Why don't you close the door dear?"

I complain to the space you once were,

shivering as wind shoulders its way

into recently-vacated rooms.
<!--more-->

<br>

The door squawks meekly, stammering in the squall,

and through the halls it thunders,

shuddering the memento-stuffed shelves.

<br>

Is this the best I can do?

Stand here shaking as the gale sails through?

I shove the door to action.

I'll close it myself.
