On a whim I decided to submit [a Flickgame poem]({{site.url}}/games/my-cats-song) to the [Flickgame Game Poems at Play Praxis](https://itch.io/jam/game-poems-play-praxis-lgf) game jam. I've only ever made Twine games before so I guess I'll have to rename that part of my site...later though! Here's the original haiku:

<!--more-->
My cat's song beckons

the birds, his chitter-chatter

thoroughly ignored.

---
Ever since I found out [how to actually write haikus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150909135830/http://haiku.insouthsea.co.uk/), I get a little frustrated at how they've been reduced to merely "5-7-5". Haikus are harder than that!

This was definitely my last poem this year, but 10 ain't bad!
