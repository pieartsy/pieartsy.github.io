A collaborative murder mystery TTRPG inspired by the world and premise of Disco Elysium (though you don't have to have played Disco Elysium to enjoy this game!)

This game is "GM-full". There is a single player character (The Detective) and four GMs (the Facets). While The Detective acts in the world and tries to solve the mystery, the Facets both create the world in a GM capacity and bicker in The Detective's mind as characters in their own right.

The mystery is collaborative - no one knows who the murderer is in the beginning, and you'll be creating Evidence and Deductions on the fly based on your creativity and the fiction you build along the way.

A Powered by the Apocalypse game that primarily takes inspiration from Bluebeard's Bride and Everyone is John.

Please note: I've gotten some good feedback from playtesters so consider this an ashcan version while I revamp the guidance and player materials!
