I ran Thirsty Sword Lesbians a while ago and my group struggled with using the Basic Moves as they are written in the core book. So, I tweaked and rewrote the Basic Moves: I changed some triggers and choices, adjusted wording, removed Moves that we were not rolling at all, and added new Moves (such as Lay Claim) that fit the theme of my specific campaign (planetary romance space pirates), and expanded the usage of the String economy. They went over well with my group over the course of several sessions, so I figured I'd share.

Note: These adjusted Moves do not play nicely with all the existing playbook mechanics; use and tweak them at your discretion.
