I'm Aster (se/er pronouns)! I'm a queer PoC with way too many hobbies. I'm a software engineer for my day job, but you'll see me in other communities: The interactive fiction forums, the Cartoonist Coop, the emergent LARP community, TTRPG spaces, and occasionally a media fandom or two. I also think I'm pretty good at poetry and pretty bad at sewing, but I enjoy both! Find me at @pieartsy almost anywhere.

If you want to chat with me, my preferred messaging platform is Discord. I drew my own custom emotes for it and I think they are funny.

Also, feel free to use my site badge if you'd like to link to this website!
